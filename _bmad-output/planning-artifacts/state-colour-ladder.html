the correction

Black is retired as a state colour. It was doing double duty as "closed now" — a healthy resting space looked like a corpse. Closed-now moves into the living green family. Dead gets the grey tombstone. They must never share a colour. The only thing allowed to read as absence is absence.

why this works at scale

MapLibre GL data-driven paint drives colour + size per point. Alive = large and bright. Seeded = small and dim. At continental density, living points self-aggregate into a glowing organism field — no separate membrane layer needed. The overview effect and the GL substrate are the same decision. And at altitude, place-names dissolve too: labels fade out, leaving only coastlines and the breathing points.

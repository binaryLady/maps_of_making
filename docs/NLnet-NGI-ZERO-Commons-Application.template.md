## KEY EVALUATION CRITERIA

Applications are scored on three weighted criteria:

1. **Technical excellence/feasibility** (30%)
   - Is the technical approach sound?
   - Is it feasible within the proposed timeframe and budget?
   - Do you have the skills to execute it?

2. **Relevance/Impact/Strategic potential** (40%) - MOST IMPORTANT
   - How relevant is it to Next Generation Internet vision?
   - What is the potential impact?
   - Strategic value for the internet ecosystem?

3. **Cost effectiveness/Value for money** (30%)
   - Is the budget reasonable for the proposed work?
   - Good value for the investment?

**Minimum score to pass:** 5.0 out of 7.0

---

## TWO-STAGE REVIEW PROCESS

### Stage 1: Initial Review
- Eligibility check
- Scoring on the three criteria above
- Projects scoring 5.0+ move to Stage 2

### Stage 2: Interactive Review
- Reviewers ask clarifying questions (typically about):
  - Differences from existing projects
  - Approach to specific challenges
  - Validation of claims
  - Collaboration opportunities
  - Budget justification
  - Sustainability plans
  - Upstream project feedback
- You have opportunity to improve proposal
- Final scoring and ranking
- Independent review committee validates selection

**Timeline:** Typically takes ~3 weeks for Stage 2

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### Writing Style
- Be concrete and specific
- Avoid marketing language
- Don't oversell or exaggerate
- Be honest about limitations
- Show technical competence
- Demonstrate awareness of ecosystem

### Budget Strategy
- Be reasonable and realistic
- Make hourly/daily rates explicit
- Include all necessary costs
- Don't inflate numbers
- Consider NLnet might suggest adjustments
- Attach detailed budget if complex

### European Dimension
- Must have clear EU connection
- Options:
  - EU-based team members
  - EU collaborators
  - Clear benefit to European users
  - Engagement with EU organizations
- State this explicitly in your application

### Open Source Commitment
- Must release all outputs as open source
- Mention specific licenses if known
- Explain how you'll make code/docs accessible
- Show commitment to community

### Reapplication Strategy
- If rejected, you can reapply in 2 months
- Calls are bi-monthly
- Learn from feedback
- Iterate and improve
- Don't give up!

## PREPARATION WORKFLOW

1. **Research Phase**
   - Review NGI vision: https://nlnet.nl/NGI/vision
   - Browse funded projects for inspiration
   - Check FAQ for specific questions

2. **Drafting Phase**
   - Write all answers offline using this template
   - Get feedback from colleagues/mentors
   - Check character counts (including spaces!)
   - Prepare attachments if needed

3. **Review Phase**
   - Self-check against eligibility criteria
   - Verify European dimension is clear
   - Confirm open source commitment is stated
   - Ensure budget is realistic and detailed

4. **Submission Phase**
   - Submit at least 1 day before deadline
   - Keep a copy of your submission
   - Save confirmation email

5. **Post-Submission**
   - Wait ~3 weeks for initial response
   - If selected for Stage 2: respond to questions promptly
   - If rejected: analyze feedback and prepare for next round
